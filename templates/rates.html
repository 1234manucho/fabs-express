How your price is calculated

We compare the actual and dimensional weight of each package, then apply the published route and service rate.

Dimensional weight = length × width × height ÷ the divisor shown on the applicable rate sheet.

Transport, fuel, handling and shipping tax are shown separately. Customs duties, destination taxes and exceptional charges are confirmed separately.
