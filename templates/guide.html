PREPARE FOR A SMOOTHER DELIVERY

Ready to ship?

Check your package, addresses and documents before submitting a shipment request.

1. Pack and measure

Use sturdy packaging suitable for the contents. Protect fragile items, seal the package and remove old labels. Weigh each package and measure its external length, width and height in centimetres.

Billable weight is calculated from the greater of actual and dimensional weight for each package. The divisor and weight increments are shown on the approved rate sheet.

2. Check collection and delivery details

Provide accurate contact names, telephone numbers, street addresses, cities, postal codes and countries. The pickup date and time window are requests until operations confirms collection.

3. Prepare cross-border documents

Describe the goods accurately and provide their declared value. Commercial goods may require a commercial invoice, country of manufacture, HS tariff code, packing list or permits. Requirements depend on the goods and route; confirm them with the operations team.

Shipping charges do not include customs duties, destination taxes or exceptional charges unless your accepted quote explicitly says so. Additional charges require confirmation before collection or further processing.

4. Check restricted goods before booking

Do not submit prohibited goods or dangerous goods through the standard booking form. Batteries, chemicals, flammable materials, controlled medicines, weapons, live animals and other regulated items can require special approval or may be unavailable for transport. Contact operations before booking any item with special handling requirements.

5. Accept your quote and pay

A submitted request is not proof of payment or confirmation that goods have been collected. Review the final quote, service conditions and exclusions. Payment is confirmed only after verification with the payment provider. Dispatch requires a verified payment.

M-Pesa requests are sent to the phone number you provide. Card details are entered on DPO’s checkout. Never share an M-Pesa PIN, card security code or account password with our staff.

6. Track movement and delivery

Shipment updates record actual operational events. Where an assigned driver provides GPS readings, the booking account can view the latest location and its timestamp. GPS may be interrupted by signal loss, device permissions or a locked phone. A map position is not a guarantee of arrival time.

Changes, cancellation and claims

Contact support with your tracking number for corrections, cancellation, a delivery issue or a claim. Charges, eligibility, carrier conditions and any refund must be confirmed for the specific booking. A support request does not itself cancel transport or issue a refund.

Final carrier conditions and liability arrangements must be included with or supplied alongside your approved shipment quotation before dispatch. FabsExpress labels and receipts do not replace a carrier air waybill, customs invoice or statutory tax invoice.
