YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy & location data.

How information submitted through this application is used.

Account and shipment information

{{ company.LEGAL_NAME }} uses the names, contact details, addresses, package details and payment references you provide to manage accounts, prepare quotes, arrange transport, process payments and respond to support requests.

Payment information

M-Pesa and DPO process payments through their own services. This application stores payment amounts, currencies, provider references and verification results. It does not collect your M-Pesa PIN or card security code, and card details are entered directly on DPO’s checkout.

Vehicle location

An assigned driver can explicitly start and stop location sharing. GPS coordinates, reported accuracy and timestamps are associated with the shipment. Exact locations are visible only to the booking customer and authorised administrators. Public tracking shows shipment milestones and city-level locations.

GPS records are scheduled for removal after {{ company.GPS_RETENTION_DAYS }} days. Driver access expires after seven days, when revoked, or on delivery. Keep private driver links and tracking numbers confidential.

Access, sharing and retention

Shipment details may be shared with the assigned transport operator and payment information with the selected payment provider for fulfilment. Account and business records are retained for operational, accounting and dispute handling purposes under the operator’s retention policy. Contact support to request access, correction or deletion and confirm applicable retention requirements.

Cookies and security

The application uses a session cookie for sign-in and a security token to protect forms. The driver page may keep its access token in session storage until the browser session ends or access is revoked. Location sharing requires your device’s permission.

Contact

For privacy requests, use the support form{% if company.SUPPORT_EMAIL %} or email {{ company.SUPPORT_EMAIL }}{% endif %}. Include your account email and the information you want us to review; do not include passwords or payment security codes.
